Help & About

Bruno Electric Estimating · offline · English

About: Offline quoting and job-costing for electrical contractors. All job data stays on this device (browser localStorage). No account and no internet required after install.

Quick start

  1. Open Company and set your shop letterhead (legal name, address, phone, email, license). Print Quote and Print Invoice use the active letterhead. Workers is a separate tab for burden rates and take-home pay estimates.
  2. Enter job header fields (quote date, Job #, customer, site address, scope). Quote date, Job #, a 15-day validity note, and the quoted-by signature appear on the Quote sheet and Print Quote.
  3. Add materials on Job materials (or pull from Catalog), then labor, equipment, and small tools on Labor & Equip. Use Reference for NEC ampacity tables (informational — does not change calcs).
  4. Set overhead and profit on Summary — that is the only margin layer for a fixed-price quote.
  5. Use Print Quote (header or Quote tab) and choose Save as PDF in the print dialog.

Company (letterheads)

Company is the letterheads vault — not the Workers roster. Letterheads are stored separately from jobs in bruno-electric-profiles-v1. Create multiple shops, set one as the active letterhead, and switch anytime from the Quote tab strip or the Company tab. Letterhead and footer on Print Quote / Print Invoice come from getActiveProfile() — not hardcoded shop strings.

  • New blank job: Clears the current job to zeros. Never deletes letterheads; active Company letterhead stays.
  • Reset demo: Restores the sample Pioneer Farms job. Does not wipe the Company vault; ensures the Bruno seed letterhead exists and prefers it as active.
  • Export / Import companies: Backup or restore letterheads only (see Help → Backup). File: bruno-companies-*.json.

Workers (burden + take-home)

Workers holds job-cost burden rates and an informational per-worker hourly pay calculator (gross / after-tax). Burden rates feed Labor & Equip and Method A bill-out. Pay calculator fields (payRate, taxRate %) do not change Quote OH&P or Labor & Equip burden. Use Export workers / Import workers on the Workers tab to move the roster without the job (see Help → Backup).

Build a fixed-price quote

  • Job materials: Quantities on this job. Extension = Qty × Unit Cost. Do not mark up materials here.
  • Catalog: Search the full materials list and add lines to the job.
  • Reference: NEC ampacity / adjustment / fill tables for lookup. Informational only — does not auto-change calculations.
  • Labor & Equip: Equipment, small tools line items (qty × unit cost, summed into Equip Cost on Summary), subcontractors, and labor hours. Burden rates come from Workers.
  • Summary (OH & profit): Adjust overhead and profit of sales (Method A). Final sales price is rounded for the base quote. This is the single margin layer.
  • Quote Total: ROUND(Summary final sales) + approved Change Orders. Next to the price: this quote is valid for 15 days from the quote date, plus Quoted by / Authorized signature for Bruno Electric personnel.

Fixed quote vs T&M invoice

  • Fixed quote (Quote, Job materials, Catalog, Labor & Equip, Summary + Change Orders): Customer price = Summary Method A (with OH & profit) + approved COs. Use Print Quote.
  • T&M Invoice: Invoice total comes from T&M equipment/labor lines plus materials and sub on that page — not from Summary OH&P. Empty detail means T&M total = $0 (honest T&M). Use Print Invoice.
  • Profit & Loss: Compare Quoted (Summary sales) vs Billed (T&M) vs Actual spend for job variance after work starts.

Change Orders

On Change Orders, add COs with amount and status. Only status approved adds to Quote Total and appears on the printed quote. Draft / submitted / rejected / void never change the customer total.

Print to PDF

  1. Click Print Quote or Print Invoice in the header (or the matching button on Quote / T&M).
  2. In the system print dialog, choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF).
  3. Letterhead and footer use your active Company letterhead. Set or switch it before printing.

Reset demo

  • New blank job: Clears the current job to zeros (job materials, labor, equipment, small-tool quantities, COs, T&M, customer fields). Keeps worker burden/pay fields, material catalog, small-tool templates, Dispatch journal, Tax settings, and the Company letterheads vault.
  • Reset demo: Restores the sample Pioneer Farms job. Does not wipe Company letterheads; ensures Bruno seed letterhead exists and sets it active.

Backup · Export / Import (detailed)

Everything stays on this device (browser localStorage) until you export a file. Exports download as .json into your browser’s usual Downloads folder (Chrome/Edge/Safari download bar). Pick a clear name and keep copies off-device (USB / Drive / email to yourself).

1) Export job / Import job (header)

  • Why: Move or archive one quote/job (materials, labor, COs, T&M, P&L, OH/profit, catalog copy on that job, workers snapshot inside the job file).
  • File name: bruno-job-<Job# or proposal>-<YYYY-MM-DD>.json (example: bruno-job-PF-2026-09-11.json). Older files may be named bruno-electric-quote-*.json — still importable.
  • Saved: Current job state (quote header, materials, labor/equip, small tools qtys, summary rates, COs, T&M, P&L, catalog embedded in the job, personnel/burden on that job).
  • Not saved: Company letterheads vault (bruno-electric-profiles-v1), theme/zoom prefs, catalog accordion open/closed. Importing a job does not replace your Company profiles list.
  • Where: Header buttons Export job / Import job. Max file size ~5 MB. Invalid JSON is rejected.

2) Export app / Import app (full backup)

  • Why: Full device backup or move to another phone/PC — shops, workers, current job, UI prefs in one file.
  • File name: bruno-app-backup-<YYYY-MM-DD>.json.
  • Saved: Job state + Company letterheads vault + UI theme/zoom (+ catalog open map). Envelope field brunoExportType: "app".
  • Not saved: Browser cookies for other sites, service-worker cache of the app shell (reinstall from the same URL if needed), files you never exported.
  • Where: Header Export app / Import app. Import asks to confirm before overwriting.

3) Export companies / Import companies (Company tab)

  • Why: Backup or share letterheads only (legal name, address, license, footer) without the job.
  • File name: bruno-companies-<YYYY-MM-DD>.json (legacy: bruno-electric-profiles.json still works).
  • Saved: Profiles vault only (profiles[], active letterhead id).
  • Not saved: Job materials, workers, OH/profit, theme.
  • Where: Company tab → Export companies / Import companies.

4) Export workers / Import workers (Workers tab)

  • Why: Move burden rates and pay-calculator wages between devices without touching the quote.
  • File name: bruno-workers-<YYYY-MM-DD>.json.
  • Saved: personnel.employees (names, gross $/hr, tax %) and personnel.burden (straight/OT burden).
  • Not saved: Company letterheads, job materials, catalog, theme.
  • Where: Workers tab → Workers backup card.

Where data is stored

On-device keys (not cloud):

  • bruno-electric-v1 — current job (includes embedded catalog + workers on that job)
  • bruno-electric-profiles-v1 — Company letterheads vault
  • bruno-electric-ui-prefs-v1 — theme + zoom
  • bruno-electric-cat-open-v1 — which Catalog categories are expanded

Clearing site data / uninstalling the PWA wipes these. Always keep an Export app file if the data matters.

Theme & Zoom

  • Theme & Zoom: Use the bottom-right controls to switch Dark/Light and adjust Zoom (saved on this device).

Dispatch · day journal

  • Where: Dispatch tab — calendar day synced to weekday and Mon–Sun week strip.
  • Each call: Time, hours, address, description, gross pay, tax % (blank = Tax settings), net after tax, status.
  • Day totals: Calls, hours, gross, tax estimate, net. Cancelled calls stay listed but week strip net skips cancelled.
  • Tax settings: Jurisdiction code/label + federal / state / local %. Effective % is stacked. Optional: apply effective % to all Workers.
  • Kept on blank job: Dispatch journal and tax settings survive New blank job (like catalog / workers).
  • Not Google Calendar: In-app day/week calendar only. Export app backup includes dispatch in the job JSON.
